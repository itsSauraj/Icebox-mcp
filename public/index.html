Connect it

Add this server to your MCP-enabled host as a connector named Icebox, using the endpoint pinned at the top of this page. Then ask for a game: “play tetris”, “quiz me on Roman history”, or just “what can I play” to see the whole grid.

No account, no data collection. Every app runs locally in the host and generates its results on the fly. See the Privacy Policy for details.
